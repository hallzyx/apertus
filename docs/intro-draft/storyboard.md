# Apertus Evidence Lab — introductory film

Draft creative direction, English narration and subtitles. Storyboard target: 112 seconds. Rendered draft: 118.27 seconds, 1920×1080, 24 fps. The separate browser walkthrough remains the demonstration of actual interaction. This film explains the research and product before that walkthrough.

## Creative direction: “Follow the claim back to the page.”

A Swiss editorial identity: warm paper (#f4f3ee), near-black evergreen (#172822), forest green (#1f5141), restrained vermilion (#bc352c), fine rules (#d5dcd4). Use the app's red square / white inset square mark, Arial or a metrically similar sans-serif, and monospace labels. Large left-aligned type, extensive whitespace, restrained motion. No generic AI brain imagery, flags, floating holograms, or cinematic stock footage. The story is a claim becoming inspectable.

A thin red line is the recurring motif: first it connects a claim to a booklet; then it passes through retrieval and the frozen model; finally it becomes the source-page underline. Use 350–500 ms crossfades and deliberate movement. Keep every scientific qualifier comfortably readable. Subtitles: max two lines, white on dark evergreen opaque/semitransparent backing, 42–48 px at 1080p, bottom safe area. Reserve the bottom 150 px. Narration should be measured, clear and natural, approximately 145 words per minute; do not rush benchmark numbers. No music is needed; an unobtrusive instrumental bed is optional only if available and licensed.

## Scientific evidence and wording rules

- Model, frozen-head methodology, training count, label mapping and multilingual contract: `track_2a/technical_report.md`, sections 1–5 and Frozen architecture.
- Final metrics and context means: `track_2a/technical_report.md`, Phase 2 Context-matched comparisons / Frozen decision; `track_2a/experiments/apertus-v15-phase2/final-results.json`; packaged `track_2a/deployment/benchmark.json`.
- Token reduction: 1 − 8059.6 / 14106.1 = 42.864%, rounded to 43%. Do not call this a 43% latency, price or energy reduction.
- Baseline .671612 is best booklet-only base validation setup with full/capped context. Original hidden head .894461 is also full/capped. Selected .949069 changes both retrieval context and matched head. Do not portray the entire improvement as a matched-context head-only causal gain. A matched hybrid-8k restricted base-score comparison is .548775 if that comparison is preferred.
- The selected head has no fresh independent heldout score. Historical 310-row .899920 belongs only to the original full/capped head; omit that score from the film to avoid confusion. The 310 split label in the methods scene must read HISTORICAL HOLDOUT, not final test.
- Grounding controls support document dependence, not proven semantic faithfulness: `grounding-results.json`, `grounding-robustness.json`, report Phase 2. Original labels are retention targets under changed premises. Keep voice qualifier “with limitations.”
- Few independent connected groups and validation-based architecture selection limit generalization. Explain in accompanying README if not spoken. No official hidden evaluation or confidence interval claim.
- Evidence is exact model-input source context, not attribution or validated minimal proof. No OCR or model weight fine-tuning occurred.
- App identity: `track_2a/src/ost_nli/web.py` CSS; actual English screen evidence in `docs/ui-review/english-desktop.png` and `docs/demo/screenshots/`. Actual recorded GPU workflow: `docs/demo/README.md`, `track_2a/experiments/apertus-live-demo-en-v1/verification.json`.
- “Jev-style” is user-provided inspiration. Repository search found no corroborating Jev citation. Do not invent a publication, author identity, architecture attribution, quote or performance number. If the intended Jev approach differs, replace that one attribution while retaining the verified description of this project.

## Editing recommendations

Keep labels in plain English; introduce NLI only in accompanying text if needed. The three labels are about the document, not universal factual truth. Neutral means the booklet does not establish the claim. The hook is an illustrative graphic, not a real evaluated claim. Use actual source text only from existing verified screenshots or demo footage; do not manufacture a source quote. Avoid tiny evidence tables. The benchmark scene needs only three bars and one cross-language callout. A brief app crop is enough here because the separate 46-second English browser recording provides the uninterrupted demonstration.

If narration runs long, target 118–120 seconds by extending scenes 23–37 and 65–82. Preserve word boundaries and subtitle synchronization; never speed up the voice to fit the initial timestamps. The timings are editorial targets, not measured speech duration.
